## The next frontier needs a ground crew.

NASA’s Moon Base plans and commercial landers are opening a new chapter of lunar exploration.

FARFIELD Robotics is developing the ground crew: robots that put instruments, tools and intelligence where missions need them.

## Reach further. Make every visit count.

- **Scout & recon.** Map routes, hazards and approaches to sites of interest.
- **Resource mapping.** Crater access. Ground-truth orbital resource maps for ISRU.
- **Supplies.** Carry instruments, equipment and tools. Free astronauts for the mission.
- **Inspection.** Revisit surface assets. Track damage, dust buildup and change.

ISRU: in-situ resource utilization, using resources found on the Moon.
